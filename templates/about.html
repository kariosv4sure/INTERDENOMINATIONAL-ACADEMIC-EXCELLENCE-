Our Journey

From Vision to Excellence

INTERDENOMINATIONAL ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE [I.A.E]™ was born from a simple but powerful vision: to provide every Nigerian student with access to quality education and admission guidance.

Founded by Mr. Daniel Moses, a passionate educator with over 12 years of experience, I.A.E has grown from a small tutorial center into a comprehensive educational ecosystem serving thousands of students across Nigeria.
