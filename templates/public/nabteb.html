Who is it for

NABTEB is usually the right call for…

Technical students

If you're studying a trade — electrical, mechanical, building, auto, welding, tailoring, catering — NABTEB is built for you.

  • National Technical Certificate (NTC)
  • Advanced National Technical Certificate (ANTC)
  • Trade-specific practical exams

Commercial students

If you're studying accounting, secretarial studies, or business — NABTEB has the corresponding certifications.

  • National Business Certificate (NBC)
  • Advanced National Business Certificate (ANBC)
  • Recognized for university admission
