IJMB vs JUPEB

Which one actually works for you?

Both are one-year A'Level programmes. Both lead to Direct Entry. The differences are practical.

IJMB JUPEB
Established 1976 — oldest 2013 — newer
Duration One year One year
Exam body ABU Zaria Consortium of universities
Recognition Federal & state universities Wider — most universities
Best for Traditional federal schools Broad national coverage
