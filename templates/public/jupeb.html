How it works

Three steps to 200 level

1

Register for JUPEB

Enrol at a JUPEB study centre (or register as a private candidate) for the one-year programme.

2

Study for one year

Attend tutorials, take the JUPEB exams at the end of the year, and earn your A'Level equivalent grade.

3

Apply for Direct Entry

Use your JUPEB result to apply for Direct Entry through JAMB. Admitted students start at 200 level.
