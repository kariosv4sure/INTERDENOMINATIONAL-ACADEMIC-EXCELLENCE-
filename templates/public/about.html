About I.A.E™

Our story.

Why we exist, what we stand for, and who's behind it. Short version: we help Nigerian students get from registration to admission without the guesswork.

Since 2016
Our journey

From one tutorial desk to a national paper trail.

INTERDENOMINATIONAL ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE [I.A.E]™ started with a simple conviction: every student deserves honest guidance, regardless of background, school, or belief.

Founded by Mr. Daniel Moses, an educator with over twelve years of experience, I.A.E has grown from a small coaching desk into a full academic services outfit serving thousands of students across Nigeria.

Our mission

Make quality academic guidance and exam preparation affordable and accessible to every Nigerian student, regardless of where they live or which school they attend. No gatekeeping. No guesswork. Just clear steps and honest advice.

Our vision

Become Nigeria's most trusted academic partner — the first name students, parents, and schools think of when it's time to move from one level to the next. Not the biggest. The one people refer their own family to.

Our principles

What we actually stand for

Not slogans. The four things we hold ourselves to on every file.

Excellence

Every registration, every coaching session, every follow-up call. Done properly or not at all.

Integrity

Clear pricing before you pay. Honest answers even when they're not what you wanted to hear.

Mentorship

We don't hand you a link and disappear. We stay on the file until the outcome is settled.

Improvement

JAMB rules change. Cut-offs shift. We update our playbook every cycle — no stale advice.

Who runs it

Founder & CEO
